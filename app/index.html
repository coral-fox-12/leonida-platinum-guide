Roadmap to the platinum

  1. Play the story first. The opening, Chapters 1–4 and the final mission unlock 6 trophies on their own, six more come from story missions along the way, and the mission strands for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone each end in one. Do the story before the grind — it unlocks the map, the activities and the money the rest needs.
  2. Watch the one missable. Positive Mental Attitude asks you to meditate at every spot across Leonida. Meditation spots are easy to walk past and never come back in a checklist, so tick them off as you travel instead of leaving them for cleanup.
  3. Sample every activity once. Master of None only needs one go at each of the 9 activity types. While you are there, start the long counts: 250 stunts, 20 different race wins, 42 Shooting Range golds, 14 Top Fun drone-race locations.
  4. Collect as you explore. Card sets, 100 vehicles, 52 areas of Leonida and 10 Scooter Bros registrations all reward simply moving through the state with your eyes open. The 100 WAiNK spots and the 5 bank vaults are the long tail — fold them into free-roam sessions.
  5. Clean up, then 100%. Finish the remaining challenges, the stranger strands and the money goals ($250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, all 8 garages). A Long Road — 100% completion — is the last gold before the platinum pops.
